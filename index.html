What is Dental Appointment Engine?

DAE combines qualification funnels, CRM automation, appointment setter workflows, and booked consultation tracking into one acquisition system for dental clinics.

Which treatments is DAE best suited for?

DAE is best suited for high-value treatments such as dental implants, cosmetic dentistry, veneers, Invisalign, and other consult-led services where qualification and follow-up matter.

Does DAE replace our front desk or treatment coordinator?

No. DAE supports your team by qualifying enquiries, triggering follow-up, surfacing priority leads, and giving setters or coordinators clearer context before they speak with patients.

How does DAE reduce lead leakage?

DAE keeps opportunities moving with fast follow-up, missed-call recovery, automated reminders, qualification logic, pipeline visibility, and clear next actions for appointment setters.

Do we need to change our CRM?

Not necessarily. DAE can be built around your existing workflow or configured inside a dedicated CRM environment depending on the clinic’s setup.
